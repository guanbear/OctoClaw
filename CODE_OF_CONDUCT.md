# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

OctoClaw uses the Contributor Covenant 2.1 as the baseline for community
behavior. This document is bilingual; the English text is authoritative if a
translation is ambiguous.

OctoClaw 以 Contributor Covenant 2.1 作为社区行为基线。本文档为中英双语；
如翻译存在歧义，以英文为准。

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺让每个人都能在本项目中免受骚扰地参与协作，不因年龄、体型、可见或不可见
的残障、族裔、性征、性别认同和表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济状态、国籍、
外貌、种族、宗教、性身份或性取向而受到区别对待。

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes
- focusing on what is best for the overall community

有助于建设积极环境的行为包括：

- 对他人保持同理心和善意
- 尊重不同意见、观点和经历
- 提供并坦然接受建设性反馈
- 对错误承担责任，并向受影响的人道歉
- 优先考虑整个社区的长期利益

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing other people's private information without explicit permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

不可接受的行为包括：

- 性化语言或图像、性关注或性骚扰
- 钓鱼式挑衅、侮辱性或贬损性评论、人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下骚扰
- 未经明确许可公开他人私人信息
- 其他在专业协作环境中可被合理认定为不当的行为

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. They may also
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

项目维护者负责解释并执行可接受行为标准。对于不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、
issue 或其他贡献，维护者可以删除、编辑或拒绝；对于被认为不当、威胁性、冒犯性
或有害的行为，维护者可以临时或永久禁止相关贡献者参与。

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and also applies when an
individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

本行为准则适用于所有项目空间；当个人在公共空间中代表本项目时，也同样适用。

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to `[项目维护者邮箱]`. Maintainers will review and
investigate reports promptly and fairly. Reports will be handled with as much
confidentiality as possible.

如需报告不可接受行为，请联系 `[项目维护者邮箱]`。维护者会及时、公正地审查和调查
报告，并在可行范围内尽量保护报告内容的机密性。

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may respond with correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent
ban depending on the impact and repeated nature of the behavior.

维护者会根据行为影响和是否重复发生，采取纠正、警告、临时禁言/禁止参与或永久禁
止参与等措施。
